Most businesses do not fail on the thinking. They fail somewhere in the middle, where nobody costed the delivery, nobody had the capacity, and nobody was accountable for it on a Tuesday morning.

Not traditional consultants

We have lived both sides.

We have worked inside complex systems, managing targets, navigating real constraints and carrying responsibility for results. We have also stepped back as advisors, to question what actually drives progress when effort is high but outcomes are not changing.

Pivot Prime exists because we have done both. We understand what it takes to move forward when plans meet pressure, people and reality.

We structure problem solving

We take problems that feel tangled and surface the full picture, so nothing important is missed. Leaders see the gaps, dependencies and risks that get overlooked when everything is treated as one big issue.

We embed operational discipline

We put structure behind execution through clear ownership, decision frameworks and practical operating rhythms, so progress does not depend on individual heroics or constant follow up.

We enable data tracking

We build practical dashboards on the data and KPIs that actually matter, so leaders can track progress, spot issues early and decide without digging through spreadsheets.

We understand human behaviour

Strategies only hold when they fit the culture, capacity and motivations of the people expected to deliver them. We work with how teams actually operate under pressure, and where resistance shows up.

The challenge

Customer onboarding and policy processing were slow and inconsistent. Teams had no visibility of workload, cost per client, or profitability at a client and transaction level.

KYC timelines were unpredictable. Leadership could not see where time and cost were being lost.

The pivot

We rebuilt operations with discipline and data at the centre.

  • Mapped end-to-end customer and transaction workflows
  • Reduced duplication and rework across compliance and operations
  • Offshored selected onboarding activities to cut bottlenecks and cost
  • Built real-time dashboards tracking cost per client, transaction time, CAC and LTV
The results
  • 67% faster transaction processing per customer
  • 10 days to 3 average KYC completion time
  • 25% reduction in operational waste
  • 2–3 FTE roles removed, holding service quality

The challenge

A proven product outside the region, priced on home-market assumptions that did not survive contact with the UAE. The commercial model was loss-making and nobody could say at what point it would stop being so.

The pivot

We built the numbers first, then the go-to-market around them.

  • Full financial model with investment, breakeven and return priced in
  • Pricing redesigned around local cost to serve
  • Go-to-market and brand localisation built for how this market buys
The results
  • 13% projected margin, from a loss-making position
  • Breakeven inside 11 months on the redesigned model
  • Nearly eighteen-fold growth in audience engagement
  • A defensible price, set on evidence rather than instinct

The challenge

Growth across two markets with an operating model built for one. Customers were being won and quietly lost, and nobody could say which service or which stage was driving the cancellations.

The pivot

We found the cancellation drivers and rebuilt around them.

  • One operating model that holds across both markets
  • Automated the reporting so issues surface before customers raise them
The results
  • 13% increase in customer retention
  • Two markets on a single operating model
  • 40% less duplicated work across the team
  • Automated reporting, replacing a manual monthly build
